I wanted to turn a 3D scan into a real-time model to practice and test some new techniques using a base mesh from 3D Scan Store, with albedo and displacement textures.

I wrapped the model with ZWrap, then used ZBrush to clean up the hair cap and correct the anatomy of the skull. I started cleaning the textures in Photoshop and finished the texturing in Substance Painter.

When 3D Scan Store released a set of blend shapes as a free ZTL, I used them to explore facial expressions and the setup process.

Overall, this was a fun study in turning scan data into a more production-ready real-time character.

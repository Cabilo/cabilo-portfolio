Abdul Alhazred, in 730 CE, amidst his visions, wrote a wretched book made from human facial leather, with ink made from blood and feces, containing profane rituals, pagan symbols and cursed languages.

It is said that to gain knowledge of the universe, you have to give in your sanity. Abdul became powerful, understood the truth of what surrounds him, but lost his mind, enslaved by the Necronomicon and serving the elder gods and their judge, the Great Dreamer, Cthulhu.

I created this character for the Flipped Normals Challenge, with the theme of Warlock, taking inspiration from other concepts and the literature of H. P. Lovecraft.
